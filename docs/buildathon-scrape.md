## Event facts
- Name: Startup Buildathon India 2026 (tagline: "Build Your Next Big Thing").
- Pitch: nationwide program for founders/aspiring entrepreneurs to turn an AI startup idea into a working product using Google AI and Google Cloud.
- Organizers on page: Google AI / Google Cloud (support alias hints "GDM x GCS" = DeepMind x Cloud for Startups), run by Hack2skill (registration, T&C, privacy, support email support+Buildathon-GDMxGCS@hack2skill.com). Apply via a Google Form link.
- Timeline:
  - 23 Oct - 15 Nov: online application (idea, solution, architecture, pitch deck). Note: FAQ says 13 Nov; hero says 15 Nov (page inconsistency).
  - Selected teams notified by 18 Nov.
  - 20-21 Nov: City Buildathons (10 cities), Day 1 ideate/design + mentor rounds, Day 2 build, submit, jury, shortlist.
  - 23 Nov Gurugram, 25 Nov Mumbai, 27 Nov Hyderabad: Regional Showcases (pitch to business mentors and investors).
  - 2 Dec: Grand Finale at Google, Bengaluru; Top 10 announced.
- City Buildathon cities: Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune.
- Eligibility: early-stage startups with a new product idea, aspiring entrepreneurs, solo or teams (no size limit); must build an AI-powered solution; no technical co-founder required; free; bring a laptop.
- Tracks/themes: no fixed tracks listed; open "AI-powered product" theme.
- Prizes: pool USD 100,000. Top 10 teams each get USD 10,000 = USD 5,000 cash + USD 5,000 Google AI and Cloud credits (subject to terms/taxes).
- Evaluation criteria: founder profile; product potential; market viability; technical architecture (scalable); depth of Google AI / Google Cloud use.
- Investor/ecosystem "show interest" form exists for showcase and finale attendance.
