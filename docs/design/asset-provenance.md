- `apps/web/public/media/road-awareness-concept.webp`: web-optimized copy of that artwork.

- Icons: the project's existing `lucide-react` dependency.
- Typography: the project's existing Plus Jakarta Sans / Google Fonts setup, with local sans-serif fallbacks.

The exact generation prompt is recorded below. No third-party customer logos, testimonials, or partner endorsement badges were added.

## Generation prompt
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape hero background for MargAI premium road-awareness website, not a UI screenshot. Create a cinematic photorealistic wide 16:9 aerial-following view of a contemporary Indian divided highway curving gently through dense deep-green hills in soft early-morning light. Camera low enough to see fine asphalt, lane markings and realistic road texture, elevated behind a single small unbranded silver car driving on the left. Road enters lower center and curves toward upper right with generous road/forest composition for subtle interface overlay. Restrained dark forest green and graphite palette, muted natural sunlight, sophisticated automotive campaign photography, crisp documentary realism. Avoid neon, sci-fi HUD graphics, all text, numbers, logos, watermarks, dramatic danger, collisions, signage text, fantasy roads. The asset is an illustrative concept backdrop; no text embedded. Produce a polished high resolution landscape image. Save final asset as an available local generated image and return its local path if supported.
